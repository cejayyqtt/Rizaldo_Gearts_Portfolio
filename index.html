Final Reflection

Looking forward: who am I becoming?

Before this course, I thought art was only for people who were good at drawing. I did not think I was one of them, so I never saw myself as a creative person. I saw art as something to look at, not something I could make.

When I look at my works together, I notice that I like using simple materials, like pages of a book, coffee, and a pen. The works I struggled with most were the ones where I could not fix a mistake easily, like carving. Those taught me to slow down and think before I act.

Carving was the most unfamiliar for me. I had to work with a hard material, and every cut stayed. It surprised me how much patience it needed. Photography was different. It made me look at small things around me and choose what to show and what to leave out.

Making art helped me notice more, ask more questions, and say things in a way that words alone could not. Poems made from book spines and blacked-out pages showed me that I can find meaning in things that were already there.

My choices show that I like quiet, simple, and hands-on work. Someone who sees my portfolio might think I am a quiet person who keeps things plain. They might misunderstand that and think I did not put in much effort. In truth, simple works took a lot of thought for me.

Looking forward, I am becoming someone who is more patient and less afraid to try something new. Art will stay in my life as a way to slow down, notice things, and express myself.
